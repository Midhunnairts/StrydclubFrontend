About Strydclub

We're building the future of sports communities in India

Our Story

Strydclub was born from a simple observation: sports bring people together, but finding the right events, teammates, and communities shouldn't be hard.

We set out to create more than just an event platform. We built a movement — a space where every athlete, from weekend warriors to serious competitors, could discover their next challenge and connect with people who share their passion.

Today, we're India's fastest-growing multi-sport community, hosting hundreds of events across running, badminton, football, volleyball, pickleball, cricket, and kho kho. But we're just getting started.
